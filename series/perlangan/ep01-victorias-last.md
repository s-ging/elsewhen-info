### Production notes

- **Silhouette design:** Lapulapu = kampilan, red-and-earth tones, bare torso, high silhouette. Humabon = layered gold-thread, ornate, seated — never stands in his own court. Spanish = full plate, but shot from below so they read as heavy and slow. Magellan is never face-forward.
- **The jar is the only recurring object in Ep 1.** Show it twice: Sugbu court, and the northbound boat. No line explains it.
- **Banquet handling:** one cup, one door, one canoe. No blood, no bodies.
- **Palette:** sea-greens and bronze for the islands; cold grey-steel for the Spanish; fire orange only in the hook and the burning.
- **Safe zone:** all text inside the 1080×1920 safe area; the date stamps sit mid-frame, never near the bottom 420px.
- **Assets to verify:** real coastline for Leyte Gulf / Mactan Channel / Bohol Strait; karakoa deck layout (raised *burulan*); lantaka profile; *Victoria* hull lines.
- **Citations to pin in the description:** Mactan date; Humabon's baptism and banquet; the *Concepción* burning; *Victoria*'s 1522 return and survivor count; the Luzon jar trade.
