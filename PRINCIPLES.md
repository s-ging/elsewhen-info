# How we work

Our working principles, written down so every session and agent works the same way. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the short, binding version; this file explains them.

## Two modes

### Brainstorm mode (the default)

For story ideas, research, scripting, storyboards and writing inside this repository.

- **Start immediately.** Ask questions alongside the first results, not before them.
- Pareto still applies: lead with what matters most.
- Nothing outside this repository changes in this mode.

### Work mode

For anything that touches a **channel or account**, a **public post or upload**, a **scheduled release**, or anything **hard to undo**.

- Run the [confirmation gate](AGENTS.md#confirmation-gate) with **at least three** clarifying questions, and wait for an explicit yes.

### Switching

Say so in one line when a session is about to touch something outside this repo. The person you are working with can also switch explicitly ("work mode", "brainstorm mode"). When unsure, it is work mode.

## Chesterton's fence

Do not remove, rename, or clean something up until you know why it is there. That includes a story beat, a title, a rule in the bible, or a dropped idea. The series already has dropped ideas (a narrator, stat cards, CHANGE tags, a living-politician story device). They are recorded with their reasons in [decisions/](decisions/) so nobody brings them back without knowing why they went.

## Pareto principle

About 20% of the effort usually drives about 80% of the outcome. For this channel the high-leverage question is: does Episode 1 hold attention to the real-timeline ending? Make it before polishing anything else.

## Credibility is the craft

The channel's protection against being mistaken for AI slop is real, specific history. Real coastlines, real dates, real names, real numbers. One wish per miniseries, and everything else follows from it. When a beat needs a new wish to work, the beat is wrong.

## Show, then tell

No narrator. The audience learns the change by watching it, and learns the real history from typed text at the end. If a shot only works with an explanation, rework the shot.
