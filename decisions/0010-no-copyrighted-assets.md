# 0010 — No copyrighted game assets; references are studied, not copied

**Date:** 2026-11-11 (recorded 2026-11-12)
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Decision

C&C Generals, Battle Realms, Warcraft 3, R.U.S.E., Civilization VI and Cities: Skylines are look references only. No assets, UI, fonts, music or voice lines from them or any other game. Everything is "inspired by."

## Why

Copyright claims and demonetization; an original look is also what protects the channel from reading as mass-produced content.
