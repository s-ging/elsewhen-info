# 0005 — No narrator, no CHANGE tags, no stat cards

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Context

Bible rev. 1 had narrator lines in the Episode 1 draft, strength and weakness cards, and an open question about a recurring host (a briefing officer, or one of Panday Pira's apprentices).

## Decision

No narrator and no host. No "CHANGE" tags and no stat cards. Episodes are told through cutscene language: letterboxed dialogue, typed location and date stamps, in-world documents and recurring objects. The real history is told at the end in typed text. Every episode works with sound off.

## Why

Show, then tell. It reads as a game cutscene rather than an explainer, and it works muted in the feed.

## Alternatives considered

- Narrator or briefing-officer host (rev. 1): dropped.
- Strength and weakness cards (rev. 1): dropped.
