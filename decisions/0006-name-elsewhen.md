**Date:** 2026-11-12

**Status:** accepted

## Decision

The channel and series are **Elsewhen** ("elsewhere" + "when"). Tagline: *history-ish*. "Perlangan" stays the in-universe nation name in the Perlangan miniseries only, never the brand.

## Why

Coined and searchable; fits every miniseries, not just the Philippine one.
