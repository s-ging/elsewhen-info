# 0003 — No living politicians, fictional dynasties instead

**Date:** 2026-11-11 (recorded 2026-11-12)
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Context

An early idea used a living Philippine political family as a story device (the "Duterte firewall").

## Decision

Permanently dropped. No real living politicians or their families appear as characters or plot devices in any miniseries. Use fictional dynasties. Historical figures dead for centuries (Lapulapu, Humabon, Panday Pira, Hideyoshi) are fair game, shown through silhouette and design, never realistic faces.

## Why

Takedown, defamation and harassment risk; it pulls the channel into current politics; it ages badly.

## Alternatives considered

- Thinly veiled stand-ins for living politicians: same problems, less honest.
