# 0004 — Japan as a 400-year ally; the WWII villain is a rogue faction

**Date:** 2026-11-11 (recorded 2026-11-12)
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Decision

In the Perlangan timeline Japan is an ally and peer. The WWII-era villain is a rogue militarist faction with an invented name and flag. Japanese characters who oppose it are on screen. Atrocity stays off-screen. The arc ends on today's warm relationship.

## Why

Keeps the story honest to the alliance the timeline builds, avoids a national villain, and keeps episodes safe for platforms and audiences on both sides.
