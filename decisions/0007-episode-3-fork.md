# 0007 — Episode 3 fork, both endings released after Episode 8

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Decision

Episode 3 (*The Sun Sets*) ends on a comment vote: *The Sun Burns* (Hideyoshi threatened, and it cost Perlangan) or *The Sun Shines* (he treated Perlangan as a peer). Both endings are produced and held, then released after Episode 8 as short bonus episodes (3a and 3b), alongside the Sulu spin-off.

## Why

The vote drives comments mid-series; holding the endings keeps the feed alive after the conclusion. Both endings converge on Tokugawa Ieyasu wanting trade, so the main line does not branch.

## Alternatives considered

- Rev. 1: release the winning ending next. Dropped; it would branch the main series.
