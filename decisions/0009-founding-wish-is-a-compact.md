# 0009 — The founding wish is a standing compact, not a single battle

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted (supersedes rev. 1's single-moment framing)

## Context

Rev. 1's rule was "one change at a time, traced to a specific moment," and Episode 1 hinged on the coalition sinking the *Victoria*. That left the warning relay and the Sugbu–Mactan cooperation as unexplained extra wishes.

## Decision

The one change is structural: a standing Tondo–Visayas maritime compact (safe passage, shared anchorages, trade in jars, iron and salt) and a shared warning network exist before Spain arrives. It is slow: days within the Visayas, months to Luzon. Everything else, including the *Victoria*'s burning, is consequence. Credibility rule 1 now reads "one structural change at a time."

## Why

One wish explains the relay, Humabon's double game, the Bohol attack and Tondo's late arrival in Episode 2. Fewer coincidences, stronger credibility.
